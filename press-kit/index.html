Biography

TiwaDara is a Nigerian singer, songwriter, producer, and sound engineer known for a soulful, groove-driven sound. Beyond performing and releasing original music, TiwaDara works behind the board — writing, producing, mixing, and mastering for other artists — and has engineering and production credits on projects for Dunnie, Deena Ade, Davina Oriakhi, Mo'believe, and Ilaye.

TiwaDara's original releases and collaborations are available across major streaming platforms including SoundCloud, Spotify, Apple Music, and Shazam.
