Biography

TiwaDara is a conduit for emotion, and a bridge between worlds — blending music, culture, and raw humanity. Through music, he channels feelings that are both universal and deeply personal, creating a space where listeners connect with themselves and their heritage.

"TiwaDara channels raw emotion through his music, blending heritage and modern sound to create something timeless and authentic."

A rising voice in the global music scene from Lagos, Nigeria, TiwaDara blends the rich, rhythmic beats of Afro-Fusion with the smooth vibes of Alternative R&B and Soul. He first emerged in 2016 with a debut single that showcased his ability to fuse soulful melodies with intricate Afrobeat rhythms, and his music often explores themes of love with eclectic production. His debut project was titled Renegade.

He is also a producer and sound engineer, with engineering and production credits on projects for Dunnie, Deena Ade, Davina Oriakhi, Mo'believe, and Ilaye.

TiwaDara is poised to become one of the leading voices in the new wave of Nigerian artists taking Afro-Fusion to the global stage — bringing a fresh perspective and an undeniable talent that continues to captivate audiences worldwide. His ability to seamlessly fuse genres and his commitment to authenticity have made him a standout artist in the Nigerian music industry, using his music as a tool for storytelling and cultural expression.

Stance on Causes & Activism

Cultural Preservation & Celebration

Protecting and elevating African culture — not just as a source of inspiration, but as a living, breathing legacy. Supporting the protection of indigenous languages, traditional arts, and cultural education while staying rooted in heritage.

Mental & Emotional Wellness

Advocating for mental health awareness, especially in African and global communities where it is often stigmatized — promoting the healing power of music and safe spaces for emotional vulnerability and expression.

Youth Empowerment & Creative Freedom

Giving the next generation the tools and confidence to express themselves authentically — supporting access to music education, creative spaces, and mentorship for young African artists, and breaking societal molds that stifle individuality or creativity.
